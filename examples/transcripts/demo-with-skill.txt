  3. Synthesis

  Observed associations (patients). Every human study retrieved that links
  clonotype persistence to outcome is correlational. Persistence of infused
  clonotypes has been documented over years: to 5 years in a single
  prostate-cancer patient after serial TIL plus pembrolizumab (PMID 41267983),
  and oligoclonally to 9.3 years after CD19 CAR T in lymphoma, where the
  dominant late clone was already present below 0.1% at day 14 (PMID 42575986).
  Where outcome is brought in, the direction is consistent — clonal expansion
  and reduced diversity tracked with clinical response in that case study (PMID
  41267983); enhanced peripheral TIL persistence accompanied response in
  advanced melanoma (PMID 42526429); the persisting dominant clone was seen in
  the complete responder in acral melanoma (PMID 41486415); clonal remodelling
  was seen in responders in a 12-patient phase I (PMID 42618927); and repertoire
  overlap between product and blood predicted response with AUC 0.79 in
  recurrent ovarian cancer (PMID 42189877). None of these designs can separate
  persistence-as-cause from persistence-as-consequence of tumour control, and
  cohorts are small (n=1 to 38).

  Causal / mechanistic evidence. The interventional work retrieved manipulates
  determinants of T-cell fitness, not clonotype persistence as such. In MC38-OVA
  mice, adjunctive LNP-mRNA antigen was required for transferred TCR-transgenic
  T cells to expand at all, with improved survival over monotherapy (PMID
  42570949) — causal for antigen-driven expansion and persistence of transferred
  cells, but using a monoclonal population, so it says nothing about
  repertoire-level clonotype dynamics. PDCD1 knockout caused edited TIL to
  retain greater clonotypic homology with the parent tumour repertoire and to
  control PDX tumours better (PMID 40753920). ASS1 or CEP20 knockout caused
  improved TIL memory phenotype, cytokine production and cytotoxicity in vitro
  and in vivo (PMID 42618927) — and those two genes were identified from a
  responder/non-responder association in the same trial, which is a useful
  example of an association followed up experimentally, though the experiment
  tested function rather than the persistence-to-response link itself.

  The gap. Across these records, no retrieved study experimentally manipulates
  clonotype persistence in patients and measures the effect on response. The
  association is reproduced across tumour types and platforms; the causal claim
  remains untested in the human setting by this evidence.

  4. Limits

  - Abstract-only basis. Full text was not retrieved for any record. Sample
    sizes, statistics, persistence thresholds, sequencing depth and follow-up
    definitions beyond what I quoted are not verifiable here. Notably: PMID
    42526429 does not state its cohort size in the abstract, and PMID 42575986's
    abstract does not say whether the 10-year persistence analysis was
    pre-specified in the registered trial — full text would need checking.
  - Not assessed but potentially relevant: PMID 42503644 (persistent
    HPV-specific TCRs in HNSCC metastasis) and PMID 42577549 (5-year TCR
    repertoire case report, anti-PD-1 + surgery) track clonotype persistence but
    not after adoptive transfer, so they fall outside the question. PMID
    41943833 and PMID 41719158 concern clonotype selection for product
    manufacture, not post-infusion persistence.
  - Search gaps. Query 1 returned 588 hits and I inspected 10 in relevance
    order; landmark earlier TIL and CAR-T clonal-tracking studies may sit
    outside that window. The retrieved set skews to 2025–2026 and to TIL
    therapy; CAR-T is represented by one paper. A query targeting an
    intervention that specifically boosts persistence in a randomised human
    comparison (e.g. IL-2 dosing, lymphodepletion intensity, or
    cytokine-armoured products) would be the way to test whether truly causal
    human evidence exists — I did not run it.
  - No result here should be read as evidence of absence; the two revised
    queries returned high hit counts that I sampled, not exhausted.
